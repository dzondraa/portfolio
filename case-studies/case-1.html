Payments Platform Scale-Up

Context

Fast-growing fintech processing millions of transactions per day faced scaling limits with a monolithic service. Failures during peak traffic windows impacted revenue and trust.

Approach

  • Domain modeling and event storming with stakeholders
  • Introduced event-driven services with idempotent processors
  • Added observability: tracing, metrics SLIs/SLOs, alerting
  • Blue/green deployments and progressive delivery

Outcomes

  • 99.99% availability across payment paths
  • 40% infrastructure cost reduction
  • MTTR reduced from hours to minutes
  • Clear ownership and faster feature delivery

Tech Highlights

  • AWS SNS/SQS, Lambda, ECS Fargate
  • TypeScript/Node, Python workers
  • OpenTelemetry, Grafana, CloudWatch
